phpMyAdmin gives database administrators and developers a browser-based interface for the everyday work of running MySQL and MariaDB — browsing and editing data, managing users and privileges, running SQL, and importing or exporting databases — without needing a separate desktop client.

It's one of the longest-running tools in the MySQL ecosystem, released under the GPL-2.0 license, and ships as a self-hosted PHP application you deploy alongside your own server.
