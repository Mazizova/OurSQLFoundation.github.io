Published by No Starch Press, MySQL Crash Course walks beginner and intermediate developers through creating databases and tables, writing queries, and using events, procedures, functions, constraints, and indexes. It closes with three end-to-end projects — a weather database, an election database protected with triggers, and a salary database secured with views — and a chapter on calling MySQL from PHP, Python, and Java.

The author, Rick Silva, is a software developer who has worked on database systems at Harvard Business School, Zipcar, and in financial services.
