Hosted by Kedar Vaijanapurkar, Deep Dive MySQL covers MySQL and related technologies in depth — from InnoDB Cluster and Group Replication to backups and performance tuning — aimed at both DBAs learning the concepts and managers who work with them. Episodes are available on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, iHeart, and Amazon Audible.
