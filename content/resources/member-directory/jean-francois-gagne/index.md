Jean-François Gagné is a System Engineer with deep experience in MySQL infrastructure, reliability, replication, automation, and large-scale operations. He worked on growing and operating major MySQL and MariaDB environments, and is now an Independent Consultant with some experience as a MySQL Open Source Developer. Jeff is known for his technical writing, speaking, and broad MySQL Community contribution. He also maintains Planet for the MySQL Community, a news and blog aggregator that helps surface activity across the MySQL ecosystem. JF is a MySQL Rockstar (2024) and recipient of a MySQL Community Contributor Award (2018).
