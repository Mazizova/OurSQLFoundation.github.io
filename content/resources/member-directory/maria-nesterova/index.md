Maria Nesterova is Managing Director at OurSQL Foundation and has over 12 years of experience in technology, including management, marketing, product development, and community programs. Through leadership roles at Percona and as CEO of Underdog Consulting, she has built teams, led products and growth initiatives, and helped open source companies strengthen communities and market presence.
