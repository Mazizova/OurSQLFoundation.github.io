Daniel Guzmán Burgos is the creator of DBTrail and a database engineer with more than 15 years of experience across MySQL, PostgreSQL, monitoring, and large-scale data platforms. He previously served as PMM Technical Lead at Percona and Head of Data Platform at Rappi, working on database observability and production operations at scale. Daniel now develops DBTrail, an Apache 2.0 open source platform that uses MySQL binary logs for investigation, recovery, point-in-time reconstruction, and analytical archives.
