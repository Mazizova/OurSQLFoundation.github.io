Tomas Ulin is Village Oracle at VillageSQL and a long-time database leader with more than 20 years of experience in the MySQL ecosystem. He previously worked with MySQL at MySQL AB, Sun Microsystems, and Oracle, including serving as Vice President of MySQL Development at Oracle. His career has focused on database engineering, product direction, and the long-term evolution of MySQL as a widely used open source database technology. He serves as Treasurer of the OurSQL Foundation.
