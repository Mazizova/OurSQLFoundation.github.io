Peter Zaitsev is Co-Founder of Percona, entrepreneur, advisor, and one of the best-known experts in open source database strategy and MySQL performance optimization. Before founding Percona in 2006, he worked at MySQL AB, where he led the High Performance Group. Peter helped grow Percona from a small consulting company into one of the most respected open source database companies, and he has continued to advise and support open source database startups and communities. He is also co-author of *High Performance MySQL*.
